Privacy Policy

Calm Color Painting  ·  Last updated: September 6, 2026

Short version: Calm Color Painting does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Calm Color Painting does not collect any personal data. We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app.

The following data exists only on your device and is never transmitted:

  • Your colored artwork (saved locally via the "Save to Gallery" feature)
  • In-app purchase status (managed by Apple, stored locally)

2. In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Unlock All") processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Storage

All data generated by the app (saved artwork images, app preferences) is stored locally on your device. This data is included in your iCloud or iTunes backup if you have backup enabled, but it is not accessible to us.

4. Third-Party Services

Calm Color Painting does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. The only external service used is Apple's StoreKit framework for in-app purchases.

5. Children's Privacy

Calm Color Painting is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else. No personal information is collected or transmitted by the app.

6. Permissions

The app may request the following device permissions:

  • Photo Library (write only): Used only when you choose to share or export your artwork. The app does not read your photo library.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
